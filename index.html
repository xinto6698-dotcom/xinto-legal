Xinto Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Effective date: To be confirmed before public release

1. Introduction

Xinto ("Xinto", "we", "us") is a business management platform that helps founders, business owners and partners organize business records, track finances, manage tasks and team members, and understand business performance. It is available as a website and as apps, and includes an AI Analyst feature.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Contact: xinto6698@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information

  • Your email address and display name.
  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic profile information Google provides for sign-in. We never see or store your password; it is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Your preferences, such as theme, date format and week start.

2.2 Business information you enter

  • Business profile: name, category, stage, currency and country.
  • Finance records, budgets and recurring entries, tasks and projects, calendar events and goals, sales, customers, inventory and Decision Lab scenarios.
  • Team data: the email addresses of people you invite, and who belongs to each business.

If you enter information about customers or other people, you are responsible for having the right to do so. Please do not enter passwords or card security codes.

2.3 Subscription and payment information

  • Your plan, subscription status, billing period dates and a subscription reference from our payment provider, Razorpay.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's page and are never received or stored by Xinto.

2.4 AI usage records

  • For each AI request we record counts, the model tier used, token counts, estimated cost and whether it succeeded, so we can enforce limits and control costs.
  • We do not store the text of your questions or the answers on our servers. The Analyst conversation is kept on your device only while the screen is open.

2.5 Technical information

Standard technical information may be processed by our hosting and authentication providers, such as device type, app version, and security and error logs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage accounts and provide the features you use.
  • To save, organize and show your business records, and to share them with the team members you invite.
  • To process AI requests and enforce usage limits.
  • To process subscriptions, renewals and cancellations.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • To respond to your requests and send important service or security notices.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. The AI Analyst

When you ask the AI Analyst a question, we send your question, recent messages from that conversation, and your business name, category, stage, currency and country to Anthropic, our AI provider, so it can generate an answer. In its current version the Analyst does not read your finance records, tasks, sales or other business data.

Standard questions use Claude Haiku 5.5, and deep analyses for Pro subscribers use Claude Sonnet 5.5. Model names may change. Anthropic processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not make claims about how long Anthropic retains data or whether it is used for training beyond what its terms state.

AI answers can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. They are general business guidance and are not accounting, tax, legal or investment advice. Check important figures and decisions yourself or with a qualified professional.

If we later let the Analyst read more of your business data, we will update this policy first and tell you what is sent.

5. Service Providers

We use these providers, which process information for the purposes described:

  • Google Firebase: hosting, authentication (email and password, and Google sign-in), database storage (Cloud Firestore) and server functions.
  • Anthropic: AI responses.
  • Razorpay: subscription payments.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own, under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Sharing of Information

  • With your team: members of a business you belong to can see that business's records and the names and emails of its members.
  • With service providers listed above, as needed to run the service.
  • When required by law, or to investigate security incidents and protect users and our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Security

We use authentication, access rules that limit each business's data to its members, and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please protect your sign-in details and tell us about suspected unauthorized access.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete your account in the app under Settings, Delete account.
  • Businesses where you are the only member are deleted with all their data. Businesses with other members must be handed over by removing the members first. When you leave a business owned by someone else, your records in it may remain.
  • If you have an active paid subscription, cancel it in Plans first so that you are not charged again.
  • Some information may remain briefly in backups, and we may keep payment and billing records as required by law.
  • AI usage records (counts and costs, not message text) may be kept for cost control and security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent where we rely on it, and make a complaint. You can export your finance records as CSV in Settings. To make a request, email xinto6698@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. Children

Xinto is intended for adults who run or help run a business and is not directed at children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

11. Subscriptions

The Pro plan costs ₹280 per month, renews automatically until cancelled, and can be cancelled at any time. Access continues until the end of the paid period. Your records are never deleted when a plan ends. Only creating more items above the Free limits is paused. Applicable taxes may apply.

12. Third-Party Links

Xinto may link to other sites, such as the payment page. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our features or providers change. We will change the date above and give extra notice where required.
